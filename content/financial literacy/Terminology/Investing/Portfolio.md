> An investor's entire collection of financial assets. 

- Includes [[Stocks]], [[Bonds]], [[Mutual Funds]], [[Real Estate]], cryptocurrency, art, and other collectibles

- Portfolio Management
	- An investing strategy based on your goals, timeline, and risk tolerance
	- Choosing what to invest in and monitoring those investments over time
- Passive management - mirror the market
- Active management - beat the market
- Things to consider when deciding how to manage your portfolio
	1. Your savings goal
	2. Your timeline (how aggressive/conservative your strategy should be)
	3. Risk tolerance
	4. How much help do you want
