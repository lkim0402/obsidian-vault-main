> Investment strategy where you divide your portfolio into different [[Asset]] classes, or categories, in order to lower risk

- Asset class
	- Grouping of investments that exhibit similar characteristics and reactions to market events
	- [[Stocks]] (equities), [[Bonds]] (fixed-income), cash and cash equivalents
- One of the most important strategy
- Selection of individual [[Securities]] is secondary to the way that assets are allocated in equities, fixed income, and cash
