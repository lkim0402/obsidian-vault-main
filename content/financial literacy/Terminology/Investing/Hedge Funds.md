>Managed funds that pool money from investors and invest in securities within multiple complex strategies in an **effort to reduce risk and maximize returns**.
- more aggressive and exclusive and mutual funds
- not regulated heavily as mutual funds, so more return and risk
- charge both a management fee and a performance fee
    - most common fee structure: two and twenty → 2% aset management fee and a 20% cut of generated gains

Strategies
- seeks profit in all kinds of markets
- investment strategies not used my mutual funds such as:
    - leverage → borrowing to increase investment exposure as well as risk
    - shorting → betting against the market
