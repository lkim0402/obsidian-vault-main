
> [!Definition]
> Any possession of significant worth that can be converted into cash according to its value

#Terminology
- **Financial asset**
	- contractual claim to something of value (future cash flows, equity, Stocks, bonds, mutual funds, and ETFs)
- **Personal asset**
	- items of present/future value owned by an individual or household
	- Cash, land, investments, personal property (vehicles, jewelry, etc)
- **Business asset**
	- Capital goods:  Material items such as machinery, buildings, and equipment used in the production of other goods and services
- When investing there are many assets to choose from: [[Stocks]], [[Bonds]], [[Real Estate]], [[Cryptocurrency]], Precious metals, Art, Collectibles, etc
