> Way for investors to minimize risk 

- A risk management strategy that mixes a wide variety of investments within a portfolio
	- One [[Mutual Funds]] can invest in hundreds (or even thousands) of individual stocks and bonds at once
	- [[ETF]]
	- Classes, [[Stocks]], [[Bonds]], real estate, commodities
- Diversifying types of investment too
	- Stocks across different sectors and industries
	- Bonds with different credit qualities, durations, and maturities
	- Investing in foreign countries
